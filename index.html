Four steps

How Toy Haven works

The shop is a front-end demonstration, so nothing is charged and no data leaves your device. The flow still mirrors a real store from end to end.

  1. Browse or search the catalogue and filter it down to one of four categories.
  2. Save what you like to your wishlist and mark it Interested, Owned or Not interested.
  3. Add to your basket and adjust quantities; totals recalculate as you go.
  4. Check out with a validated form and keep the order in your local history.
